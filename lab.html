UNIT II OUTLINE

Muscle naming and movement

Action terms

Flexor decreases a joint angle; extensor increases it. Abductor moves away from the midline; adductor moves toward it. Supinator turns the palm anteriorly or superiorly; pronator turns it posteriorly or inferiorly.

Name clues

Longus = long; brevis = short; maximus = largest; minimus = smallest; major = larger; minor = smaller; rectus = straight; oblique = diagonal.

Movement roles

Agonist = prime mover; antagonist opposes the action; synergist assists a larger agonist or stabilizes a joint.

Attachments and action

Origin is typically the less movable attachment; insertion is typically more movable. The action describes the movement produced when the muscle contracts.
